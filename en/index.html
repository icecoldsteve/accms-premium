This is what a roadmap looks like

A Flemish creator, 90 days, platform by platform. Reach first via Instagram and TikTok, then revenue via OnlyFans and Fansly, then the secondary channels.

Instagram

High priority · day 1–30
  • Bio and link-in-bio with one clear call-to-action
  • Daily 1 Reel + 1 carousel and 5–8 Stories
  • Trending audio, strong hooks, pin the best Reel
  • Belgian identity and behind-the-scenes as the common thread

KPI · +15–25% followers per month · link clicks · Story engagement

TikTok

High priority · day 1–30
  • Claim or create the account
  • 1–3 short clips a day, repurpose the best Reels
  • Personality, humour and visual hooks
  • Captions in Dutch and English

KPI · 2–3 viral clips · profile visits · link traffic

OnlyFans

Core revenue · day 1–45
  • Bilingual bio (Dutch + English)
  • Test a price increase to €9.99–€12
  • Clear customs menu and weekly PPV bundles
  • 4–5 posts a week, the occasional live session

KPI · subscriber growth · revenue per fan · customs volume

Fansly

High priority · day 15–60
  • Set up the profile and mirror core content
  • Use Fansly's stronger discovery
  • Test different PPV pricing than on OnlyFans

KPI · second revenue stream · lower acquisition cost

MYM

Medium · day 20–60
  • Strong in the Benelux: local-language content
  • Cross-promotion with local creators

Role · local audience, complements OnlyFans

X · F2F · YouTube Shorts

Supporting
  • X: 3–5 posts a week with direct links
  • F2F: scheduled live sessions with higher tickets
  • Shorts: repurpose the best clips

Role · traffic, engagement, extra discovery

Own website
